### **About:**
Vanilla Recipes Reforged is a quality of life expansion, aiming to make small improvements to Minecraft in its crafting live. Features aim to enhance the game whilst still maintaining the vanilla experience. Inspired by many small other datapacks, and a few own ideas :)

Future Updates Note:
I try to update the pack when i can! No discord for now.

CB=Craftback
